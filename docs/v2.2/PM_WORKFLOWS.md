# Product Management workflow templates

## Meeting to backlog
Inputs: authorized transcript/notes, participant list, agenda, existing PRD/backlog snapshot. Outputs: cited summary, observed decisions vs proposed decisions, owners/dates with uncertainty flags, draft actions, draft epics/stories. Gates: user review of minutes and explicit external tracker-write approval.

## Competitive research to roadmap
Inputs: research question, region/date range, permissible sources, business goals. Outputs: source log, competitor comparison, opportunity hypotheses, evidence strength, prioritization with explicit assumptions, draft roadmap. Gate: human priority decision.

## Requirements to engineering
Inputs: approved PRD version, constraints, nonfunctional goals, scope and risks. Outputs: epics/stories, executable acceptance criteria, traceability IDs, engineering handoff. Gate: approved exact artifact version, existing engineering orchestrator and PR review.

## Analytics to product decision
Inputs: approved dataset, schema, KPI glossary, cohort definitions. Outputs: reproducible calculations, confidence/limitations, hypotheses, experiments and recommendation. Gate: approval for production data access and any resulting backlog write.

## Universal output rules
Cite provenance for nontrivial claims; flag uncertainty; do not invent meeting participants, operator metrics, jurisdictions or market facts; preserve confidential content access scope; never treat an LLM summary as a formal stakeholder approval.
